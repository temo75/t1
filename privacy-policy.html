TEMO DEI Bill Sync — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

TEMO DEI Bill Sync helps a user find DEI electricity-bill messages in their own Gmail account and display extracted billing details and bill history on their private T1 account page.

Google data we access

When you choose to connect Google, the app requests Gmail read-only access (gmail.readonly) and permission to identify the connected Google account. The app searches Gmail for messages from DEI, reads relevant message content, and extracts information such as bill amount, due date, payment status, and message date.

How we use and store data

Google account email addresses are transformed into account-specific keys. OAuth refresh tokens and extracted bill records are stored in Cloudflare Workers KV so the app can refresh access and maintain bill history. Short-lived T1 session tokens are stored as hashes. The app uses the data only to provide the bill lookup, display, history, and synchronization features requested by the user.

Access tokens are handled by the server and are not displayed in the page. The app does not send, modify, or delete Gmail messages. It does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

Sharing and service providers

Data is processed by Google APIs to provide Gmail access and by Cloudflare infrastructure to run the Worker and store app data. We do not sell or share Gmail data with advertisers. We do not use Gmail data for purposes unrelated to the bill features described above.

Retention and revoking access

Stored bill history and the refresh token may remain in the app's storage until deleted. You can revoke the app's Google access from your Google Account's third-party connections/security settings. Revoking access stops future authorized access but may not automatically erase data already stored by the app. To request deletion of stored app data, contact temo75elia@gmail.com and identify the Google account used to connect; do not send passwords or tokens.

Security

The app uses OAuth authorization, server-side token handling, account-specific storage keys, and short-lived sessions. No internet service can guarantee absolute security; do not share OAuth tokens or passwords.

Children

This service is not designed for children under 13.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the service changes. Questions or deletion requests can be sent to temo75elia@gmail.com.

Google API Services User Data Policy: The app's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
